# Prompts des assets actuels

Création avec l’outil intégré imagegen. Références : logo et photos originales du bar. Les trois prompts ci-dessous ont produit les masters de cette livraison.

## 1. cabane_v3_hero_prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: cinematic full-bleed website hero campaign image for the real French beer bar La Cabane à Mousse, Herbignac. Input 1 is its supplied logo, reproduce its exact cabin/beer mug orange yellow white identity on physical bottle labels. Inputs 2 and 3 are actual bar photographs, use them as atmosphere and architecture reference: dark charcoal industrial beer bar, metal high chairs, warm wood counters, amber wooden pendant lamps. Primary scene: extraordinary close wide-angle editorial moment of FOUR adult friends (clearly aged 30-45) clinking FOUR chilled amber beer bottles, candid joy, realistic diverse hands and faces, one clearly visible warm laughing adult woman and a male friend behind the bottles in soft focus. Bottles converge naturally near x=1000 y=520 in a 1536x1024 landscape scene, hands enter from right edges and lower right, necks just touching, not breaking, no spilling or splash. Bottle bodies dominate right 60%, exact original logo labels clearly facing camera on each bottle, plausible paper campaign label applied to amber glass. Left 38% is dark atmospheric uncluttered charcoal negative space for live website headline. Friends and background occupy right half, genuine warm indoor bar evening lighting, small sunlight glow evoking sociable summer evenings, authentic wood and metal pub, absolutely no tropical plants, no beach, no vintage cinema props. Dramatic macro condensation, glass refraction, sophisticated flash photography meets atmospheric warm tungsten, premium editorial photo with amber glints, shallow depth of field, subtle analog film grain. Minimal palette from venue: #191918 charcoal, natural brown wood, golden beer #F3B43F, warm white foam and restrained orange label ribbon. No new slogans/text outside bottle labels, no other brand names, no fabricated exact reproduction of premises, campaign is conceptual. Keep left region readable at low brightness. No faceless disconnected impossible hands, no extra fingers, four bottles only, upright believable toast. 1536x1024.

## 2. cabane_v3_cutout_prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied final hero campaign. Produce a transparent animation layer with EXACT same 1536x1024 canvas, pixel registration, same bottle scale and positions. Keep ONLY the three amber beer bottles touching necks in the upper-right-center, their three hands/forearms naturally attached: woman's right hand and arm entering from left of bottle group, two right-side hands holding the middle-front and rear-right bottles. Preserve every bottle label exactly, original lighting, condensation, fingers, ring/wristwatch and clothing sleeves. Remove all faces, torsos not attached to arm sleeves, walls, lights, counter, other objects and entire background to true alpha. DO NOT center, enlarge, move, recreate, redesign labels, add fourth bottle or change contact point. Contact between bottle necks remains around x1070 y240. The foreground layer will be overlaid at same position on source photograph for a subtle cinematic parallax. Full-size registered transparent PNG, not a collage, no border.
